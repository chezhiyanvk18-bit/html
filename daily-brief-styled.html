Free Bus Trial Lifts Ridership by 18% in River City

More people are leaving their cars at home since fares were removed on 12 routes.

By Meera Krishnan | Published on 19 November 2026

More people in River City are taking the bus since fares were dropped on 12 routes in May. The Municipal Transit Authority says weekday ridership on these routes rose by 18 per cent over six months.

Key facts

  • The trial started on 1 May 2026.
  • Ridership is up 18 per cent from last year.
  • The council will vote on the scheme in January.

Ridership on the busiest routes

Route Riders in 2025 Riders in 2026
Route 7 8,200 10,100
Route 12 6,400 7,500
Route 21 5,100 5,900

Supporters say free buses reduce traffic. Critics say the city loses too much money. The council will decide next year.
